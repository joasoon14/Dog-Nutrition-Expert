How the process works

A clear and individual approach

1. Initial assessment

We begin by discussing your goals, current situation, challenges, available information, and the type of support you need.

2. Evaluation

I evaluate the recipe, product concept, ingredients, production method, or the dog's current feeding plan and individual needs.

3. Recommendations

You receive practical recommendations, calculations, and clear next steps based on the agreed scope of the project or consultation.

Individual recommendations consider the dog's age, weight, body condition, activity level, current diet, food preferences, digestive tolerance, and any relevant veterinary information.
