Individual guidance

Support based on your dog's needs

Individual nutrition guidance is suitable for dog owners who want professional help understanding, evaluating, or changing their dog's diet.

It may be helpful when you are starting raw or home-cooked feeding, reviewing an existing diet, switching to a different type of food, or trying to better meet your dog's individual nutritional needs.

Nutrition guidance

Areas I can help with

Current diet review

Evaluation of your dog's current food, ingredients, feeding amounts, meal structure, and overall nutritional suitability.

Raw & home-cooked feeding

Guidance on raw and home-cooked diets, including ingredient selection, meal composition, and nutritional balance.

Commercial food selection

Help comparing air-dried, freeze-dried, fresh, and other foods, understanding labels, and selecting suitable products for your dog.

Feeding amounts & meal planning

Practical feeding recommendations based on your dog's age, size, body condition, activity level, current diet, and individual needs.

Food transition

Guidance on introducing a new food or feeding method gradually and monitoring your dog's response.

Individual nutritional needs

Nutrition support for dogs with individual dietary needs, provided alongside veterinary care when appropriate.

Before the consultation

Information I will need

To prepare for the consultation, please provide as much information as possible about your dog and their current diet.

  • Your dog's age, breed, sex, and current weight
  • Body condition and activity level
  • Current food, treats, supplements, and feeding amounts
  • Daily feeding schedule
  • Food preferences and foods your dog avoids
  • Digestive tolerance and any recent dietary changes
  • Relevant veterinary diagnoses or test results
  • Your main questions and goals for the consultation

How it works

Consultation process

1. Send your request

Contact me with a short description of your dog, their current diet, and the main questions you would like help with.

2. Diet review

Before the consultation, I review the information you provide about your dog's diet, feeding routine, and individual nutritional needs.

3. Consultation & recommendations

During the consultation, we discuss the diet review, your questions, practical recommendations, feeding options, and suitable next steps for your dog.

More detailed nutrition planning

Need a complete individual feeding plan?

Some cases require additional work outside the consultation, such as detailed nutritional calculations or the development of a complete individually formulated raw or home-cooked feeding plan.

If additional formulation or calculation work is needed, the scope and price will always be agreed with you in advance.

Health conditions

Nutrition support alongside veterinary care

Nutrition can be an important part of supporting dogs with diagnosed health conditions. However, nutrition consultations do not replace veterinary examination, diagnosis, monitoring, or medical treatment.

When a dog has a diagnosed or suspected health condition, relevant veterinary information may be needed before dietary recommendations can be prepared. Collaboration with the dog's veterinarian may also be appropriate.
