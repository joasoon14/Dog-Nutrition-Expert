How the process works

A clear and project-specific approach

1. Initial assessment

We begin by discussing your product, goals, current development stage, available ingredients, production method, challenges, and the support you need.

2. Technical evaluation

I evaluate the relevant recipe, product concept, ingredients, nutritional composition, processing method, or other technical aspects of the project.

3. Recommendations & deliverables

You receive practical recommendations, calculations, documentation, or other agreed deliverables based on the scope of the project.

Business projects

Support tailored to your product and production goals

Projects can range from the evaluation of a single existing recipe to the development of a completely new product concept. The scope is agreed individually according to the product type, available ingredients, production method, target customer, and required technical support.

Before the project begins, we define the objectives, deliverables, available information, expected timeline, and scope of the work.
