Business services

How I can support your product development

Product & recipe development

I develop new dog food recipes, treats, and complementary feeds based on your product idea, available ingredients, and intended production method. From initial formulation to recipe adjustments, the goal is to create a product that works in practice.

Nutritional evaluation & balancing

I calculate and evaluate the nutritional composition of dog food recipes, including comparisons with relevant FEDIAF guidelines. I can identify nutrient gaps or excesses and suggest adjustments to improve the nutritional balance.

Ingredient selection

Choosing the right ingredients involves more than looking at their nutrient content. I can help compare raw materials based on nutritional value, functionality, cost, availability, and suitability for your production process.

Recipe review & reformulation

Already have a recipe? I can review its nutritional balance and ingredient composition, identify possible issues, and look for ways to improve the formula, simplify ingredients, or reduce costs without losing sight of your product goals.

Production & product quality

Processing can change the nutritional composition and characteristics of a finished product. I can help evaluate how drying, heating, and other processing steps affect the formulation, and support production- and quality-related questions during product development.

Technical product information

I can help prepare and review feeding guidelines, nutritional information, product specifications, and technical documentation based on formulation calculations and available analytical data.

Functional product concepts

Functional ingredients with a purpose

A functional ingredient should have a useful role in a recipe, not just look good on the label. I have worked with ingredients such as seaweed, psyllium, bamboo fibre, algae and herbs, considering their effects on fibre content, texture, processing and nutritional balance.

I can also help develop products with specific nutritional goals, including digestive health, skin and coat, mobility, healthy ageing and cognitive support. Ingredient selection depends on the recipe, its intended use and the available scientific evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before starting a project

Can you evaluate a recipe against FEDIAF nutritional guidelines?

Yes. I compare calculated nutrient levels with the relevant FEDIAF guidelines for the intended life stage, using the food's actual energy content. I identify possible gaps or excesses, but the accuracy depends on the ingredient data and may require laboratory confirmation.

Can you adapt a recipe to a specific production method?

Yes. Raw, fresh, air-dried and freeze-dried foods have different formulation needs. I consider moisture changes, ingredient behaviour and possible nutrient changes during processing.

What do you need to start a project?

A short description of your product idea, existing recipe or main challenge is enough to start. We can discuss ingredients, production methods, available data and any restrictions after the first contact.
