Depending on the nature of your enquiry, this information may include:

  • Your name

  • Information about a product or development project
  • Information about your dog

Where your enquiry relates to a potential or existing service, the processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual measures or performance of a contract.

4. Dog nutrition consultations

If you request an individual dog nutrition consultation, I may ask you to provide information necessary to evaluate your dog's current diet and nutritional needs.

This may include information such as your dog's age, breed, sex, weight, body condition, activity level, current food, feeding amounts, supplements, food preferences, digestive tolerance, veterinary diagnoses, or veterinary test results.

Information relating to your dog is processed for the purpose of preparing and providing the requested nutrition service. Personal information relating to you as the client is processed in connection with the preparation and performance of the contractual relationship.

If you contact me regarding pet food product development, formulation, technical consultation, or another business service, I may process information about you, your company, your professional role, and the project you describe.

This information is processed for the purpose of evaluating your enquiry, preparing an offer, communicating about the project, and, where applicable, performing the agreed service.
