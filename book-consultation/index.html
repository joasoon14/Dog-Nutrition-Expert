Who is it for?

Personal guidance for your dog's diet

An individual consultation is suitable for dog owners who want professional help understanding, evaluating, or changing their dog's diet.

The consultation may be helpful when you are starting natural feeding, reviewing an existing diet, changing food type, or trying to better meet your dog's individual nutritional needs.

Consultation topics

Areas we can discuss

Current diet review

Evaluation of your dog's current food, ingredients, feeding amounts, meal structure, and overall nutritional suitability.

Natural feeding

Guidance on raw, fresh, air-dried, freeze-dried, and other minimally processed feeding options.

Individual meal planning

Practical feeding recommendations based on your dog's age, size, body condition, activity level, and preferences.

Food transition

Guidance on introducing a new food or feeding method gradually and monitoring your dog's response.

Ingredient selection

Help understanding ingredients, food labels, product differences, and which options may suit your dog.

Individual nutritional needs

Nutrition support for dogs with specific needs, developed alongside veterinary care when appropriate.

Before the consultation

Information I will need

To prepare for the consultation, please provide as much information as possible about your dog and their current diet.

  • Your dog's age, breed, sex, and current weight
  • Body condition and activity level
  • Current food, treats, supplements, and feeding amounts
  • Daily feeding schedule
  • Food preferences and foods your dog avoids
  • Digestive tolerance and any recent dietary changes
  • Relevant veterinary diagnoses or test results
  • Your main questions and goals for the consultation

How it works

Consultation process

1. Send your request

Contact me with a short description of your dog, the current diet, and the main questions you would like help with.

2. Complete the assessment

You will provide the information needed to evaluate your dog's diet and individual nutritional situation.

3. Receive recommendations

We discuss the findings, practical recommendations, feeding options, and suitable next steps for your dog.

Consultation format

What is included?

The exact scope depends on your dog's situation and the type of support required. A consultation may include:

  • Review of the information you provide
  • Evaluation of the current diet
  • Individual feeding recommendations
  • Food or ingredient selection guidance
  • Suggested feeding amounts and meal structure
  • Practical next steps
  • A written summary of the main recommendations

More complex cases may require additional information, veterinary test results, or a separate follow-up consultation.

Health conditions

Nutrition support alongside veterinary care

Nutrition can be an important part of supporting dogs with diagnosed health conditions. However, nutrition consultations do not replace veterinary examination, diagnosis, monitoring, or medical treatment.

When a dog has a diagnosed or suspected health condition, relevant veterinary information may be needed before dietary recommendations can be prepared. Collaboration with the dog's veterinarian may also be appropriate.
