About the expert

Food science applied to canine nutrition

I help pet food businesses develop better products and support dog owners in making informed decisions about natural feeding.

Kerit-Lii Joasoon

MSc Food Science and Food Technologist

I am a food technologist with a Master's degree in Food Science and professional experience in dog food development, recipe formulation, ingredient selection, nutritional evaluation, production, and product quality.

My specialist focus is natural and minimally processed dog food, including raw, air-dried, freeze-dried, fresh, and other animal-based feeding formats.

I work with both pet food businesses and individual dog owners. For businesses, I provide support with product concepts, formulation, nutritional balancing, ingredient selection, production considerations, and existing recipe evaluation.

For dog owners, I provide practical nutrition guidance based on the dog's age, activity level, body condition, current diet, preferences, and individual needs.

My approach

Science-based, practical, and individual

Evidence-based

Recommendations should be grounded in nutritional science, accurate calculations, ingredient knowledge, and a clear understanding of the individual dog or product.

Natural nutrition

My work focuses on high-quality animal ingredients and minimally processed feeding approaches, while also considering nutritional balance, safety, and practicality.

Individual solutions

There is no single feeding solution for every dog or every business. Each project is considered according to its goals, limitations, target customer, production method, or the individual dog's needs.

How it started

A personal interest that became a profession

My interest in canine nutrition began with my first dog, Alassie, who was never particularly enthusiastic about regular kibble. This led me to explore different feeding approaches and learn more about how food can influence a dog's digestion, coat, wellbeing, and quality of life.

That interest eventually led me to study food science and build professional experience in dog food development. Today, I use that combination of scientific education, product development experience, and practical knowledge to support better feeding decisions and better dog food products.
